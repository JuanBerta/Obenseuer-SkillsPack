=== NAME ===
Skills Pack

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (summary) ===
Eight skill mods in one: Scavenging, Haggling, Bribery+, Stealth, Fraud (Scam Artist), Lockpick Learning, Woodcutting and XP Tiers. Made with AI assistance. Real skills on the Character tab with XP, levels and level 10 perks. Replaces the separate mods; saves and settings carry over.

=== FILE DESCRIPTION ===
Obenseuer-SkillsPack-v1.3.5.rar : Skills Pack 1.3.5 for BepInEx 6. Extract into the Obenseuer folder. Delete the old separate skill mods first.

=== CHANGELOG ===
1.3.5
- Fix: Bribery+ did nothing on game versions that lack the CharacterHeadController.PlayerMadeNoise method (it threw an error every time you pressed the ask key). It now skips that step when the method is missing.
- Made with AI assistance (Claude).
1.3.4
- Fix: the Bribery+ ask key (B) and the Scam Artist ledger key did nothing on non-English keyboard layouts (for example Russian). Hotkeys now read the physical key, so they work on any layout. The log now shows the ask key and the keyboard layout at start-up.
- Made with AI assistance (Claude).
1.3.3
- Fix: lowering Woodcutting's MaxLevel no longer cuts your stored level. MaxLevel now only caps the effects; the stored level is kept.
- Fix: Woodcutting progress now also has the never-decreasing backup the other skills have (JuanBerta.Obenseuer.SkillsPack.progress.txt), so a missing, older or reset woodcutting.json cannot lose it.
- Fix: the progress backup file is replaced in one step, so it cannot be left missing if the game closes while it is written.
- Made with AI assistance (Claude).
1.3.2
- Woodcutting now levels up by XP like the game's own skills: 1000 XP per level (XpPerLevel), 10 XP for each axe hit (XpPerHit) and 40 XP each time a tree or log gives wood (XpPerWood). The Character tab shows your total XP, and the row appears from your first chop, with a message.
- Woodcutting: drinking makes some hits clumsy misses (DrunkMissChance) that do little but give 3x XP (DrunkMissXpFactor), and weaken your chopping and bonus wood (DrunkOutputLoss). Mushrooms raise your effective Woodcutting level while you are high (MushroomsBoostSkill).
- Old Woodcutting settings XpPerLevelStep and XpPerChop are replaced; your Woodcutting level and XP from 1.3.1 carry over (the XP now counts toward the 1000 XP levels).
- Made with AI assistance (Claude).
1.3.1
- Woodcutting now shows in the Character tab (level and progress to the next level), like the game's own skills. The Home key is gone.
- Made with AI assistance (Claude).
1.3.0
- New: Woodcutting skill (moved in from the separate Woodcutting mod). Chopping trees and logs with an axe levels it up to 10 by default (MaxLevel is a setting): the axe hits harder and swings faster, and each tree or log gives extra wood. Each save slot keeps its own level (Saves/<character>/woodcutting.json); press Home to see it. Own on/off switch, and a warning in the log if the separate Woodcutting.dll is still installed.
- Made with AI assistance (Claude).
1.2.3
- Bribery+: success odds rise faster with level (ExtraSuccessPerLevel 3%/level on top of the game's 3%, about 90% for a stranger at level 10, never above 95%) and rewards grow faster toward level 10 (RewardPerLevelSquared: 13.1x the level-1 reward at level 10 instead of 4.6x)
1.2.2
- Bribery+: if the game does not report the person you are looking at, the mod now looks straight ahead from the camera to find them. When the ask key finds nobody, the reason is written to the log (the cursor is free, too far, nobody in front of you...), so a key that does nothing can be diagnosed.
1.2.1
- Fix: skill progress could be lost after a reload (a small load between the building and the street, a death, loading an older save). Every skill now keeps a second copy of its XP in memory and in BepInEx/config/JuanBerta.Obenseuer.SkillsPack.progress.txt; a load that brings back no data or less XP than that copy keeps the higher value. Progress only goes up (delete a line of that file to reset a character by hand).
- Fix: on the stable game build (0.5.08) a game method that does not exist no longer stops the rest of a skill's patches: it is skipped with a warning in the log. Scavenger's extra finds are skipped there if the game lacks the method they need.
1.2.0
- New: XP Tiers. Recipe skill XP scales with the value of the result compared with the other recipes of that skill (4 tiers: x1, x1.5, x2.5, x4 by default; cutoffs and per-recipe overrides are settings), planting included. Mining, jobs, sauna, drinking and furniture blueprints give several times more XP, each with its own multiplier. Own on/off switch.
1.1.0
- New: Lockpick Learning. The game's Lockpicking skill grows faster from a lockpick that fails (0.8 of a success instead of the game's 0.2) and faster again when you are drunk or high (up to double). Both amounts are settings; it has its own on/off switch.
1.0.0
- First release: Scavenger, Haggling, Bribery+, Stealth and Scam Artist in one mod.
- Scam Artist: frozen bank accounts unfreeze on their own after 7 in-game days.
- On/off switch per skill.

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (BBCode) ===
[b]MADE WITH AI:[/b] this mod was written with the assistance of AI (Claude by Anthropic) under my direction. The source code is public.

[size=5][b]Skills Pack[/b][/size]
[i]Skill mods for Obenseuer in one download.[/i]

This pack replaces my separate mods [b]Woodcutting[/b], [b]Scavenger[/b], [b]Haggling[/b], [b]Bribery+[/b], [b]Stealth[/b] and [b]Scam Artist[/b]. Every skill shows on the Character tab like the game's own skills, starts unlearned, and uses the game's "New skill learned" and level-up popups.

[size=4][b]The skills[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Scavenging[/b]: search trash containers to level up. Higher levels dig up extra, and better, items. Masks and a crowbar help; darkness without a light hurts. [i]Level 10, Trash King:[/i] a chance of rare finds.
[*][b]Haggling[/b]: every trade with a trader gives XP. Up to 12% off what you buy and 12% more for what you sell. [i]Level 10, Silver Tongue:[/i] traders buy stolen goods from you.
[*][b]Bribery+[/b]: look at a person and press [b]B[/b] to ask for money, using the game's Bribery skill. People differ, police pay more but are harder, and failing can offend them or get you reported.
[*][b]Lockpick Learning[/b]: the game's own Lockpicking skill grows faster from failed attempts (0.8 of a success instead of 0.2) and when you are drunk or high (up to double). Both amounts are settings. [b]Added in 1.1.0.[/b]
[*][b]XP Tiers[/b]: recipes give more skill XP the more valuable their result is: the cheapest recipes of a skill stay at normal XP, the top ones give up to 4x (planting too). Mining, jobs, sauna, drinking and furniture blueprints give several times more XP, so they are worth levelling with. Every multiplier, the tier cutoffs and per-recipe overrides are settings. [b]New in 1.2.0.[/b]
[*][b]Woodcutting[/b]: chop trees and logs with an axe to level up (level 10 by default, a setting). Your axe hits harder and swings faster, and every tree or log gives extra wood. Each save slot has its own level. Your level shows in the Character tab. [b]New in 1.3.0.[/b]
[*][b]Stealth[/b]: XP for stealing, lockpicking and trespassing unseen. People and cameras notice you less (up to 50%), more so when crouching or in the dark. [i]Level 10, Shadow:[/i] crimes you're caught for count as less serious.
[*][b]Fraud (Scam Artist)[/b]: scam invoices play out over days. See your odds and payout on each invoice, read the companies' replies in the scam ledger ([b]F7[/b]), send follow-ups, and bribe, settle or lay low when reported. [b]New:[/b] a frozen bank account unfreezes on its own after 7 in-game days.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Coming from the separate mods[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Delete the old DLLs[/b] from BepInEx/plugins (Obenseuer-Scavenger, -Haggling, -BriberyPlus, -Stealth, -ScamArtist, and Woodcutting.dll). The pack warns in the log if one is still there.
[*][b]Your saves carry over:[/b] skill levels, XP and the scam ledger.
[*][b]Your settings carry over:[/b] each skill keeps its own config file.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][i]JuanBerta.Obenseuer.SkillsPack.cfg[/i]: an on/off switch per skill (restart needed). A switched-off skill keeps its progress.
[*]Each skill's numbers are in its own file ([i]JuanBerta.Obenseuer.Scavenger.cfg[/i], [i]...Haggling.cfg[/i], and so on), or in ConfigurationManager.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Requires BepInEx 6 (be.697 or newer).
[*]Works with Loot Map (searched-trash markers), Special Orders (Haggling bonus) and Diegetic UI.
[*]Don't install it together with the separate skill mods.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
Extract the archive into the Obenseuer game folder. The DLL ends up in [i]BepInEx/plugins/Obenseuer-SkillsPack/[/i].
