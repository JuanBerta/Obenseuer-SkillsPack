Obenseuer Skills Pack 1.0.0 (BepInEx 6)
=======================================

Scavenger, Haggling, Bribery+, Stealth and Scam Artist in one mod.

Install: extract into the Obenseuer folder (needs BepInEx 6 be.697 or newer).
The DLL goes to BepInEx\plugins\Obenseuer-SkillsPack\.

IMPORTANT: if you have the separate mods, delete their DLLs from BepInEx\plugins
(Obenseuer-Scavenger, -Haggling, -BriberyPlus, -Stealth, -ScamArtist).
Your saves, skill levels and settings carry over.

Switch skills on/off: BepInEx\config\JuanBerta.Obenseuer.SkillsPack.cfg
Each skill's own settings: BepInEx\config\JuanBerta.Obenseuer.<Skill>.cfg
